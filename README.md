# ^_^ The Protocol

## This Mod is all about following the Rules to Live — You follow the rules and You Live.

## You Break it and You Die. Follow the Protocol No Matter What.

---

# ^_^ The Start

You can start the challenge by typing the command `/start` in the chat.

---

# ^_^ The Rules

Once the challenge starts, a random rule is chosen and lasts for **30 seconds**. When the time is up, a new random rule takes its place **(never the same one twice in a row)**.

The current rule and the time left are shown on your screen.

| Rule | What you must do |
|------|------------------|
| **No Sprinting!** | Don't sprint. Walk only. |
| **No Jumping!** | Keep your feet on the ground. |
| **No Breaking!** | Don't break blocks. Blocks that break instantly, like grass and flowers, are allowed. |
| **Don't Move Your Head!** | Keep your camera still. No looking around. |
| **Kill a Mob!** | Kill a mob before the time runs out, or you die. |

# ^_^ The Strikes

You don't die right away. You get a few chances:

<img width="854" height="480" alt="The Protocol - Strikes" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/998b54c0-41d7-4725-b375-146219590ba5" />

- **Grace Period:** The first **3 seconds** of a rule are a warning only. Nothing happens to you.
- **Strikes:** After that, every time you break the rule, you get a strike, a warning on your screen, and a short penalty such as **Slowness, Nausea, or Mining Fatigue**, depending on the rule.
- **Death:** You get **2 free strikes**. The **3rd time** you break the rule, you die.
- **Kill a Mob:** This rule is different. There are **no strikes**. If you haven't killed a mob when the time runs out, you die.

When a rule ends, you'll see how many times you broke it.

---

# ^_^ The End

You can stop the challenge by typing the command `/stop` in the chat.

---

# ^_^ Requirements

- **Fabric Loader**
