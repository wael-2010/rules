# <span style="color:green">  ^_^  The Protocol  </span>

## <span style="color:cyan"> This Mod is all about following the Rules to Live , You follow the rules and You Live  </span>
## <span style="color:cyan"> You Break it and You Die, Follow the Protocol No Matter What</span>

<hr style="border: 4px solid navajowhite;" />

# <span style="color:green">  ^_^  The Start  </span>

## You can Start The Challenge by Typing The Command /Start in the Chat.

<hr style="border: 4px solid navajowhite;" />

# <span style="color:green">  ^_^  The Rules  </span>

## Once the challenge starts, a random rule is chosen and lasts for 30 seconds. When the time is up, a new random rule takes its place (never the same one twice in a row). The current rule and the time left are shown on your screen.

| Rule | What you must do |
|------|------------------|
| **No Sprinting!** | Don't sprint. Walk only. |
| **No Jumping!** | Keep your feet on the ground. |
| **No Breaking!** | Don't break blocks (blocks that break instantly, like grass and flowers, are allowed). |
| **Don't Move Your Head!** | Keep your camera still. No looking around. |
| **Kill a Mob!** | Kill a mob before the time runs out, or you die. |

# <span style="color:green">  ^_^  The Strikes  </span>

## You don't die right away, you get a few chances:

<img width="854" height="480" alt="2026-10-02_18 40 11" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/998b54c0-41d7-4725-b375-146219590ba5" />


- **Grace period:** the first 3 seconds of a rule are a warning only. Nothing happens to you.
- **Strikes:** after that, every time you break the rule you get a strike, a warning on your screen, and a short penalty (slowness, nausea or mining fatigue depending on the rule).
- **Death:** you get 2 free strikes. **The 3rd time you break the rule, you die.**
- **Kill a Mob** is different: there are no strikes. If you haven't killed a mob when the time runs out, you die.

## When a rule ends, you'll see how many times you broke it.
